Audio to Text
📲 Install app
Use Audio to Text like a phone app. It opens without internet after installing.
Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

Choose an audio file, then press Transcribe. The first run downloads the speech model.
