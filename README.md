# BLO Forms

Fill Form 6, 7 and 8 office papers and download them as one PDF.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io

# GSWS Forms

Fill ward / village secretariat application forms (income, caste, rice card, birth, marriage and more), preview them on the official form, and download as PDF or Excel.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/gsws/

# Learn English

English vocabulary for exam practice: A–Z words, words often confused, one-word substitutes, idioms, phrases, phrasal verbs and roots, with a daily exam of 20 questions per section.

Dictionary data: Open English WordNet (CC BY 4.0), derived from Princeton WordNet.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/english/

# Audio to Text

Turn an audio file or a microphone recording into text (English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil) on your own device, then save it as PDF or text.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/audio/

# Itihasam – Indian & AP History

Indian and Andhra Pradesh history for APPSC Group 1 and Group 2: 61 chapters with NIOS-based briefs, connected revision points, mind maps, tables, location maps and the NIOS book's figures; kings and kingdoms in order with order games; 30 daily Prelims questions, mock tests; and 122 Mains questions with 150/250-word model answers.

Figures and maps: NIOS Senior Secondary History (311).

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/history/

# House Expenses

House construction expense book: add materials and labour with quantity, rate, paid amount and shop, see budget, category and stage totals and shop balances, and save online so the entries come back on any phone.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/house/

# Adhyayam – Chapter Study Packs

Study packs for APPSC Group 1 and Group 2 chapters: short notes, tables, mind map, keywords, analysis, a memory paragraph, tests and a revision plan. Chapters are built in Claude and saved as pack files; this app opens them, works offline and tracks revision.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/adhyayam/

# Paper Register

Stock and usage register for secured certificate papers: enter each bundle received (first number and count), then record service, application and printed date against every paper number; shows balance, damaged papers, and prints or exports the register.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/paper/

# Medicine Guide by Age & Disease

For registered medical practitioners in AP: choose male/female, age, weight, pregnancy, search and pick one or more of 90 diseases with severity to see medicines with weight-based doses, a combined prescription, interaction and referral warnings. Upload or type blood/urine reports and X-ray findings to get suggested diseases. Includes malaria, TB, child dose chart and newborn/pregnancy/elderly tables.

Open the app: https://bloforms.github.io/medicine/
