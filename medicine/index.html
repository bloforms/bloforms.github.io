Malaria — NCVBDC drug policy (AP: ACT-SP)


Treat only after RDT/smear is positive. P. vivax: Chloroquine 3 days + Primaquine 0.25 mg/kg daily × 14 days. P. falciparum: Artesunate 3 days + SP on day 1 + Primaquine 0.75 mg/kg single dose on day 2. Mixed: ACT-SP + 14-day Primaquine.

Approximate weight by age


2 mo ≈ 5 kg · 6 mo ≈ 7 kg · 1 yr ≈ 10 kg · 3 yr ≈ 14 kg · 5 yr ≈ 18 kg · 8 yr ≈ 25 kg · 10 yr ≈ 30 kg. Formula (1–10 yr): weight ≈ (age × 2) + 8 kg.










Newborn & young infant (0–2 months)


Most sick young infants need referral after a first dose. Keep warm, continue breastfeeding, refer.




ProblemPre-referral / OPD treatment


Possible serious bacterial infection (fever ≥37.5 °C, poor feeding, fast breathing ≥60/min, chest indrawing, convulsions)First dose Inj. Gentamicin 5 mg/kg IM (7.5 mg/kg if over 7 days) + oral Amoxicillin 50 mg/kg, then refer to SNCU


Fast breathing only (7–59 days, feeding well)Oral Amoxicillin 50 mg/kg/dose twice daily × 7 days (PSBI protocol, if referral refused)

Pregnancy & breastfeeding




Generally safeAvoid / contraindicated


Paracetamol, Amoxicillin, Cephalexin, Cefixime, Azithromycin, Nitrofurantoin (not at term), Metronidazole (after 1st trimester), Chloroquine, Cetirizine, Salbutamol & Budesonide inhalers, Labetalol, Nifedipine, Methyldopa, Insulin, Levothyroxine, IFA, Calcium
Telmisartan & all ACE inhibitors/ARBs, Atorvastatin, Ciprofloxacin, Doxycycline/Tetracycline, Primaquine, Ivermectin, Albendazole in 1st trimester, ACT in 1st trimester, Valproate, Warfarin, NSAIDs after 20 weeks, Misoprostol




Breastfeeding: most of the safe list is fine; avoid Primaquine (if baby's G6PD unknown) and high-dose Aspirin.






Elderly (60+)


  • Start low, go slow — often half the adult dose
  • Avoid: Glibenclamide, long-term NSAIDs, Chlorpheniramine/Promethazine (confusion, falls), Diazepam
  • Review all medicines every 3 months; check kidney function before Metformin, NSAIDs, Telmisartan






Medicines NOT to give children


  • Aspirin under 16 years (Reye's syndrome)
  • Cough/cold combination syrups (Chlorpheniramine + Phenylephrine) under 4 years
  • Loperamide and other anti-diarrhoeal 'stoppers'
  • Ciprofloxacin/Levofloxacin except dysentery per IMNCI
  • Tetracycline/Doxycycline under 8 years
  • Nimesulide under 12 years (banned)
  • Promethazine under 2 years
  • Oral Salbutamol syrup










Refer immediately — do not treat at OPD


Child


  • Not able to drink or breastfeed
  • Vomits everything
  • Convulsions
  • Lethargic or unconscious
  • Chest indrawing with stridor
  • Severe dehydration
  • Severe pallor
  • Young infant with temperature ≥37.5 °C or below 35.5 °C


Adult


  • SpO2 below 94%
  • BP below 90/60
  • Altered sensorium
  • Bleeding (dengue)
  • Jaundice with fever
  • Severe malaria signs
  • Chest pain


Not for self-medication. Unqualified practitioners should refer, not prescribe. Antibiotics only when clinically indicated.
